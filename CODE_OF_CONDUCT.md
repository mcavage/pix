# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people. No harassment,
no discrimination, no personal attacks.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other channel where you are acting as part of the pi-stack community.

## Standards

Encouraged:

- Direct, specific, technical feedback.
- Assuming the other person is trying to help.
- Owning your mistakes and moving on.

Not tolerated:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via the contact on the
[@mcavage](https://github.com/mcavage) GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, and may block contributors who
break these rules.

This is a lightweight adaptation of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1. See
that document for the full text and rationale.
